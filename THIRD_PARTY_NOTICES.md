The icon subset contains 15 glyphs and has been renamed internally to **RPN
Contained Icons** to respect the original font's Reserved Font Name. The included
Font Awesome notice also describes its other distributions; this plugin bundles
only the font subset, not its JavaScript or SVG distributions.

The [font manifest](fixtures/sock-pile/fonts/manifest.json) records source URLs,
hashes, families, weights, and preparation details. The renderer embeds only
validated packaged files; it never downloads fonts while rendering a post.

The Sock Pile comparison uses third-party photos, illustrations, and original
forum posts locally. Those images and post contents are not distributed here.
